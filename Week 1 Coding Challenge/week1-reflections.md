*AI Development Residency · Week 1 Homework*

## What I Did

I have spend the whole week thinking about the topic for my residency project. As you know, I totally got into the rabbit hole, coming up with the 5-6 ideas, and trying to jump to the solutions during my exploration. After countless days circling around, I finally went back to thinking only about the problems and what am I passionate about, and was able to choose a topic that I am currently curious about, interested to learn more, and also care deeply. I actually created a *problem scoping* skill to help me write problem statements, which helped tremendously to figure out what I care the most.

After I chose my topic, I have researched Advanced Agentic Workflows and AI Ethics topics with Claude, revising the paper and learning along the way. There were not many technical things for this assignment, but I did a few prototypes of the product I have envisioned to work out some details in my mind (all assumptions!!!) to include in the research paper.

## What I Learned

Next time, I would start from the problem, and not going to jump to the solutions. Next time, I will also remember that product work is an iterative process, and what I have envisioned right now will most certainly change. I definitely spend way too much time and tokens discussing the topic and being in my rabbit hole, so what I have learned is that I can work much more efficient if I don't get too much into my head, not try to control everything, or make waterfall plan. It's definitely a learning process and the mindset plays a big role. I have learned that the vision is so much more important than technical specs, and that I should trust my judgement and my intuition as I am moving forward with developing the product, it's all assumptions until I validate those with data, so I also learned to let things go and not trying to make everything perfect from the beginning as it's the opposite of what is needed.
